# Existing style anchor

Maintain the current arcade sports direction: bright ice, saturated team jerseys,
dark broadcast panels, condensed display typography, gold rewards, orange fire.
Tokens live in `src/ui/styles.css`: ink #0c0c14, ice #eaf3ff, red #ff2d3a,
blue #2f6bff, gold #ffd23f, fire #ff7a1a.

Judge captures for puck visibility, player/team separation, legible clock and score,
camera coverage of play, consistent panel typography, and useful hit/goal feedback.
Check reduced-motion and non-color cues when touching those effects. Preserve
asset provenance; generated assets need inspection in the actual scene.

Players use smooth, rounded anatomy and detailed hockey equipment, not a low-poly
art style. Preserve team readability with curved jerseys, helmet shells/visors,
segmented gloves, skate laces, and stitched goalie pads. Keep the existing rig and
deterministic gameplay separate from asset detail.

For each visual iteration retain before/after images at the same viewport, quality
and scenario. Record concrete violations and fixes with image paths. Do not invent
a numerical style pass or reference images that have not been inspected.
