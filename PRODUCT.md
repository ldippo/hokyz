# Hokyz

## Register

product

## Users

Arcade hockey veterans, first-session players, and roguelite players. They play
short matches on keyboard or gamepad, then choose rosters, perks, and routes.
Existing audience perspectives are recorded in docs/design/personas.md.

## Product Purpose

Responsive, readable arcade hockey with satisfying hits and meaningful run
choices. The current goal is graphics polish and substantially better gameplay.

## Brand Personality

Physical, exuberant, competitive. Smooth, detailed hockey players rather than
low-poly characters, with PBR materials, reflective ice and a punchy grade.
The user's current character direction supersedes historical v2 proportions.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic technical demo, unreadable spectacle, and progression that becomes
chores. These concerns are documented in the game contract and v3 persona reviews.

## Design Principles

- Keep the puck and the next action readable at game speed.
- Make hits, passes and goals satisfying without interrupting player agency.
- Give roster, perk and route choices a visible effect on play.
- Present short, navigable screens that work from the couch or keyboard.
- Preserve the existing arcade identity while improving its craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve remapping and live input glyphs, keyboard/gamepad navigation, larger text,
non-color cues and colorblind palettes, and reduced motion. These are shipped
features, not optional polish. No additional compliance level is asserted.
