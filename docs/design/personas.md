# Review perspectives

- First-session player: can I find the puck, learn the next action, and recover
  from a loss? Watch navigation, prompts, and readability.
- Arcade hockey veteran: do checking, passing, shots, fire, and goal feedback feel
  responsive and satisfying? Screenshots alone cannot establish timing or feel.
- Roguelite player: do roster, perks, route, and rival choices change my strategy?
  Check choices and run-state evidence, not just arena screenshots.
- Accessibility player: can I distinguish cues without color, remap controls, and
  play with reduced motion and larger text?

Record prefer-before / prefer-after / abstain plus concrete evidence for each
relevant perspective. Missing evidence means abstain. A single agent's perspective
passes are self-review, not a blind independent panel or real player research.
